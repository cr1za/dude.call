# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — Floating button control and usability fixes

- Compact FLOATING ON/OFF control below the fullscreen arcade button.
- OFF immediately removes overlay; UNDO is available for three seconds.
- Notification STOP and drag-to-REMOVE keep floating mode OFF until manually enabled.
- 64dp transparent floating button; one short tap calls, long-press and drag repositions it.
- Free positioning without edge snapping; positions persist across restarts and adapt to rotation.
- Overlay permission explanation with OPEN SETTINGS/CANCEL; no unsolicited launch prompts.
- Requests Android 13+ notification permission once when enabling the service.
- Shared five-second cooldown across fullscreen, home-screen widget, and floating button.
- Accessible floating-button click action, first-finger touch tracking, small overlay touch area.
- Modern Android Back handling and improved service lifecycle.
- Transparent home-screen widget still calls immediately with one tap.
